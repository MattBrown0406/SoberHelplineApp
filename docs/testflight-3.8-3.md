No private answers are transmitted to invitations.

## Privacy and release boundaries

- Practice progress is account-scoped local device storage, not encrypted storage or cross-device sync. Reset clears personal wording while retaining an opaque revision marker so stale screens cannot restore it.
- Reopening completed practice does not automatically trigger an invitation; saved results remain free.
- Urgent help stays free; no paid access grants, backend policy changes, subscription trials or testimonials are added.
- This release is for EAS and internal TestFlight, not public App Store review, OTA, production website or Supabase rollout.
- Physical-device notification delivery, OS share/print and StoreKit transactions are separate from automated verification.
